Forces

  • Left drag the force tool at the 3D cursor, Shift inverts it
  • Q W E attract, repel, swirl
  • [ ] or Alt + wheel cursor depth
  • Ctrl + click pins an attractor, Backspace X remove

Camera

  • Right drag, arrow keys or two fingers orbit
  • Middle drag or Shift + arrows pan
  • Wheel, pinch or + − zoom
  • Drag the compass, or click an axis to look along it
  • C reset, F follow, A auto-rotate

Keys

  • Space pause, . step
  • R reseed, M morph
  • 1 to 9 presets
  • H hide everything, Esc back
  • Shift + F full screen, ? this card
