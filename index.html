Controls

Left drag
Apply the force tool at the 3D cursor. Hold Shift to invert it.
Right drag
Orbit the camera
Middle drag
Pan (or Shift + right drag)
Wheel
Zoom. With Alt, or [ ], move the cursor deeper.
Ctrl+click
Pin an attractor. Backspace removes the last, X clears all.
Keys
Space pause, . step, R reseed, M morph, F follow, C reset view

1 to 9
